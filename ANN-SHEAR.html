ANN-SHEAR

ALTECAD CONCRETE — ribbon panel ANNOTATION

Automatic annotation of reinforced concrete shear walls with their thickness.

How to use
  1. Choose the drawing scale [25/50/100] (the last value is remembered).
  2. Select the closed polylines on layer SHEAR-WALLS.
  3. The thickness is computed automatically and the text is oriented along the wall.
Notes: Polylines must be closed LWPOLYLINE on layer SHEAR-WALLS. Previous annotations are cleaned before redrawing.
