ALIGNER

ALTECAD CONCRETE — ribbon panel STRUCTURE

Aligns a selection of objects on the edge or axis of a reference object. Supports rotated objects (oriented bounding box).

How to use
  1. Select the objects to align → Enter.
  2. Click the reference object.
  3. Choose the alignment mode (default: Left): Left, Right, Top, Bottom, CX (center horizontally), CY (center vertically).
Notes: Requires full AutoCAD (not LT) — ActiveX / Visual LISP is needed.
